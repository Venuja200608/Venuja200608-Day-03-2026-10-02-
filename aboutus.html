About Us

Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SpaceX) is an American aerospace manufacturer, provider of space transportation services, and satellite communications corporation. Founded in 2002 to revolutionize space technology, SpaceX designs, manufactures, and launches advanced rockets and spacecraft. Through breakthrough developments in reusable rocket technology—most notably the Falcon 9, Falcon Heavy, and Starship programs—SpaceX has drastically reduced space transportation costs, making space travel and commercial satellite deployment significantly more accessible and reliable.

Our Mission

To enable human life on other planets and make humanity multiplanetary. At SpaceX, we believe a future where humanity is out exploring the stars is fundamentally more exciting than one where we are not. We are dedicated to pushing the boundaries of aerospace engineering, expanding satellite global coverage via Starship and Starlink, and building the necessary infrastructure to establish a self-sustaining human presence on Mars and beyond.SpaceX's mission is to enable humans to become a multi-planetary species by making space travel more affordable and sustainable. The company is actively working on the development of the Starship spacecraft, which is designed to carry large numbers of passengers and cargo to destinations such as Mars and beyond. By advancing space technology and infrastructure, SpaceX aims to establish a permanent human presence on other planets, ensuring the long-term survival of humanity.
